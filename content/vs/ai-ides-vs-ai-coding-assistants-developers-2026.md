The landscape of developer tools has been radically reshaped by artificial intelligence. For many of us, the question isn't *if* we'll use AI, but *how* and *where* it integrates into our workflows. This article cuts through the marketing noise to provide a practical comparison between two evolving paradigms: AI IDEs (or, more accurately, IDEs with deeply integrated AI) and specialized AI coding assistants. If you're a developer looking to optimize your productivity in 2026, understanding these distinctions is crucial for making informed choices that genuinely enhance your daily work.

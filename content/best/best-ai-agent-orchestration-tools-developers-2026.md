The landscape of AI development has rapidly evolved beyond single-shot LLM calls. Modern applications increasingly leverage multiple AI agents, each specialized for a particular task, collaborating to achieve complex goals. This shift introduces significant challenges: managing agent interactions, sequencing tasks, handling state, ensuring robust communication, and maintaining overall system coherence. AI agent orchestration tools address these challenges by providing frameworks and platforms to define, execute, monitor, and scale multi-agent workflows. They are crucial for moving from experimental AI scripts to production-grade intelligent systems.
